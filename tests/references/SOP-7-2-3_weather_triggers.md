# SOP 7-2-3 — Weather Triggers for Outdoor Training

**[FICTIONAL TEST DATA — for planner-mcp testing only]**

## 1. Purpose

Establishes mandatory pause and modification criteria for outdoor training
based on weather conditions. Applies to all 1-100 IN BN training events
conducted outside hardened structures.

## 2. Scope

Covers: night land navigation, daytime land navigation, mounted operations,
weapons familiarization, and field-craft exercises. Does NOT cover indoor
classroom or simulator-based training.

## 3. Visibility thresholds

| Visibility | Action |
|---|---|
| ≥ 1000m | Normal operations. |
| 500-999m | Increase cadre-to-trainee ratio for navigation exercises (1:6 minimum). |
| 200-499m | PAUSE non-essential outdoor lanes. Land navigation REQUIRES escort. |
| < 200m | SUSPEND all outdoor training except force protection / shelter movements. |

## 4. Lunar illumination thresholds (night training only)

| Illumination | Action |
|---|---|
| ≥ 30% | Standard. |
| 15-29% | Add reflective markers to route checkpoints; cadre with NVDs. |
| < 15% | PAUSE land navigation unless trainees individually equipped with NVDs and a 1:4 cadre ratio is met. |

## 5. Precipitation

- **Light rain:** May continue; ensure trainees have appropriate wet-weather gear.
- **Heavy rain (>0.25"/hr):** PAUSE all field training pending battalion S-3 review.
- **Thunderstorms within 10nm:** SUSPEND all outdoor training. Move to shelter.

## 6. Pause vs Suspend

- **PAUSE:** Hold trainees at known checkpoint, await improvement, resume when below trigger.
- **SUSPEND:** End training event. Recover all trainees to assembly area. Reschedule.

## 7. Documentation

All weather-triggered pauses or suspensions WILL be documented in the
training event after-action review with: trigger met, time of decision,
decision maker, and resumption decision (if pause).

## 8. Authority

Pause and Suspend authority rests with the senior cadre on site or above.
Battalion S-3 may direct retroactive Suspend if SOP violation is identified
in after-action review.
